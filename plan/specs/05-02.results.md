## Consolidations so far

- `TestBlockedDaemonStatus`, `TestPauseResumePreservesLatch`, `TestRestartPreservesLatch`, `TestControlStaleToken409`, and `TestBlockedDaemonDoesNotServeIndexIntegration` are subtests of `TestBlockedFolderStatusAndLatch`, sharing one latched daemon; their assertions are retained.
- `TestConfirmDuringDownloadIntegration` and `TestOldRunnerCannotCommitAfterConfirm` are represented by `TestConfirmRejectsOldDownload` cancellation and late-write subtests; both stale publication checks are retained. The concurrent-confirm and stop/drain races remain separate.
- `TestV2HelloRequired`, `TestV2FileAndIndexWireFormat`, `TestNamedIndexRefresh`, and `TestAckAuthenticationAndValidation` are subtests of `TestV2SharedFixture`, with ACK mutation last.
- `TestDeltaServerRejectsMalformedAndMutation` is the final subtest of `TestDeltaServer`; targets use separate names and are scanned together. Invalid block-size and block-hash requests moved from the successful-case loop into that subtest, while stale-size checks remain per case.
- `TestFilesChangesBetweenFrames` and `TestFilesBoundedSequentialBodies` are subtests of `TestFilesStreaming`; bounded delivery uses two maximum-size bodies and runs before the change cases. Each change case has distinct filenames and the latch-setting case runs last.
- `TestEncodeTextUsesGzipForRelay` and `TestEncodeJPEGUsesIdentity` are table cases in `TestEncodeBodyFormats`.
- Delta pipeline modes use distinct random content and filenames in one initial scan per folder, retaining direct, relay, and local-edit assertions.

Risk inventory in owned packages:
- Data survives interruption or competing steps: `TestBoundedLogWriterRecoversAfterRotationFailure` checks the original log bytes after a failed rename and successful retry; `TestStopFolderCancelsAndDrainsBlockedDownload` checks an existing file survives cancellation of an in-flight download; `TestDeltaPeerPipeline/local_edit` checks the receiver edit remains after an in-flight transfer; `TestConfirmRejectsOldDownload`, `TestConcurrentConfirmDrainsDownload`, and `TestStopFolderCancelsAndDrainsBlockedDownload` check stale publication and drain behavior.
- A transient error must not become a tombstone: `TestRunPeerRetriesOnlyFailedBodyWithoutBaselineRefetch` now holds the retry after a 500 response and checks the destination has no tombstone, then verifies the retried bytes. This explicit assertion is the single owned-package check identified for this risk.
- Concurrent paths: the confirmation, stop/drain, watcher, and `TestActivityRace` tests remain for race execution.
- Malformed/incompatible wire: `TestBatchCodec*`, `TestDeltaServer/RejectsMalformedAndMutation`, `TestFetchDeltaValidation`, and protocol-version tests retain rejection checks; symlink, mutation and cancellation cases remain separately covered.
- The four `TestAckRetriesDroppedRequestWithoutRemoteChanges`, `TestAckRefreshesStaleBaseline`, `TestAckBaselineAllowsIgnoredLiveFile`, and `TestAckBaselineRequiresIgnoredDeletionCoverage` cases survive as subtests of `TestAckRetryScenarios`; independent folders are scanned concurrently before the sequential protocol cases.
- `TestBlockedFile` and `TestBlockedIndexAfterLongPoll` are subtests of `TestBlockedFileAndLongPoll`; the latter establishes the latch for the file check.
- `TestIndexLimits` is represented by `TestIndexStreamingRejectsLimitAndMalformedRecords`; its unique oversized-vector case was added to the existing table, which already rejects over-limit bodies and long names.
